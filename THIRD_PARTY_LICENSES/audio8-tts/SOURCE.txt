Audio8 TTS (https://github.com/Audio8-AI/Audio8_TTS, Apache-2.0)

The add-on's engine (audio8_addon/engine.py) and text front end
(audio8_addon/text.py) are ports of upstream's ONNX runtime,
onnx_runtime/arktts_runtime/{runtime,prompt,registration}.py at commit
1d64afcc2da56222b41ddcf53de7b8bc54be1fb6, changed for the add-on: a pure-Python
tokenizer, a cap on a line's length, cached voice registration.

The model weights are not in this archive; they download on the first line,
all Apache-2.0: the codec, the tokenizer and Preview 0.6B from
Edge0/Audio8-TTS-Preview-0.6B-ONNX-INT4, and Realistic 0.6B from
stephvax/audio8-tts-realistic-0.6b-onnx-int4, an independent ONNX export of
Edge0/Audio8-TTS-Realistic-0.6b, on Hugging Face.
LICENSE and NOTICE here are upstream's.
